# OpenVOGE Brand Identity & Assets

Official vector assets, logos, typography, and brand identity guidelines for the **OpenVOGE** ecosystem.

All assets are pure vector files (SVG) and high-resolution renders (PNG with 100% transparent alpha channel), completely free of background rectangles and artificial effects.

---

## 🏍️ Official VOGE Reference

This identity is built directly on the geometry of the official VOGE automotive emblem:

- [`original/voge.svg`](original/voge.svg) – Vector source of the official VOGE emblem and logotype.
- [`original/voge.png`](original/voge.png) – High-resolution raster reference.

## 📐 Color Palette

| Color | Hex | RGB | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Electric Cyan** | `#00D2FF` | `0, 210, 255` | Primary open-source accent, `open` wordmark, telemetry flow |

## 🔤 Typography

- **`open`**: Clean geometric sans-serif (DIN 1451 / Bahnschrift) in lowercase.
- **`VOGE`**: Exact official OEM automotive bold geometric vectors from Loncin/VOGE.
